## 🌈 What is ScoutOps AI?

**ScoutOps AI** is a smart company research system that turns public company data into a simple business opportunity signal.

> 🌐 **Find → 🤖 Understand → 📊 Measure → 🎯 Decide**

Instead of manually opening many websites, reading company information, checking business numbers, and comparing opportunities, ScoutOps AI brings these steps into **one automated flow**.

---

## 🔴 The Problem

Company research is usually:

- 🔴 Too much manual searching
- 🔴 Information spread across different sources
- 🔴 Difficult to compare companies
- 🔴 Business numbers separated from company information
- 🔴 Hard to know whether the information is reliable
- 🔴 No clear way to measure the final opportunity

---

## 🟢 The Solution

ScoutOps AI combines **company intelligence + business metrics + external discovery + evidence**.
💡 What does it actually find?

ScoutOps AI can identify:

🏢 What the company does
🎯 Who it serves
👥 Who leads it
📧 Public contact points
📊 Business performance signals
💎 LTV / CAC relationship
📈 Growth & retention signals
🔎 Additional web information
💼 LinkedIn discoveries
🛡️ Evidence & quality
🎯 Opportunity & strategic fit

🚀 Where can it be used?
